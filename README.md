# Credit Repair Tracker (beta)

This is where I share beta versions of **Credit Repair Tracker**. It's a Windows app I made to keep my own household's credit records organized: credit reports, disputes and their legal deadlines, collector contacts, letters, and a pay-down plan. Your records stay on your computer.

It isn't legal advice. It's a tool I made for myself and decided to share. You decide what to do and what to send.

## Download

**[Get the latest version](https://github.com/maconner85/credit-repair-tracker-beta/releases/latest)**: download the `.zip` under Assets, unzip it, and open **Read me first.txt**. It runs on Windows 11 or later.
